## Why per-repo, not per-developer

We're not competing with Copilot — we're competing with governance-tier tools like Snyk and Semgrep. Repos are the right unit because agent activity scales with repos, not with developer headcount. A team running one nightly bot on five repos is the ideal Team customer at $1,500/month.
